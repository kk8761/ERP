# Product Overview

NexaERP is a production-grade SaaS ERP platform designed for small businesses, starting with grocery stores and expandable to pharmacies, restaurants, hostels, and schools.

## Core Value Proposition

Free, investor-grade ERP platform that provides enterprise-level functionality for modern small businesses without the complexity and cost of traditional ERP systems.

## Target Users

- **Primary**: Small business owners (grocery stores, pharmacies, restaurants, hostels)
- **Secondary**: Managers, cashiers, and staff members with role-based access
- **Future**: Schools and other educational institutions

## Key Modules

- **Dashboard**: Real-time KPIs, revenue charts, and alerts
- **POS/Billing**: Touch-friendly point-of-sale with GST invoice generation
- **Inventory**: Full CRUD operations, barcode scanning, stock alerts
- **Orders**: Sales and purchase order management
- **Accounting**: Income/expense ledger, P&L statements
- **CRM**: Customer management with loyalty points
- **Staff & Payroll**: Employee management and salary processing
- **Analytics & Reports**: Revenue trends, GST reports, inventory exports
- **AI Assistant**: Natural language business insights

## Business Model

- **Current**: Free tier with demo capabilities
- **Planned**: Multi-tenant SaaS with subscription billing
- **Future**: B2B marketplace and API integrations

## Deployment

- **Frontend**: Netlify static hosting (live at nexaerp.netlify.app)
- **Backend**: Node.js/Express API (production-ready architecture)
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM
