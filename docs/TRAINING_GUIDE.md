# NexaERP Enterprise User & Administrator Training Curriculum
Requirements: 28.3, 28.4

---

## 1. Role-Based Training Tracks

### Track A: System Administrators & SecOps
- **Module 1**: RBAC, User Onboarding, and JIT Role Elevation Governance.
- **Module 2**: Disaster Recovery, Monthly Drills, and Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR) Execution.
- **Module 3**: 90+ Day Retention Lifecycle Management and WORM Archiving.
- **Module 4**: Monitoring, Performance Optimization, and System Diagnostics.

### Track B: Finance & Accounting Operators
- **Module 1**: Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries, and Double-Entry Auditing.
- **Module 2**: Fiscal Year Closing, Multi-Currency Revaluation, and Tax Engine Setup.
- **Module 3**: Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and Bank Reconciliation.

### Track C: Supply Chain & Warehouse Managers
- **Module 1**: Inventory Operations, Multi-Warehouse Transfers, and Stock Movements.
- **Module 2**: Predictive Stock Depletion Analytics and Reorder Point Automation.
- **Module 3**: Barcode Scanning Integration and Mobile Warehouse Receiving.

### Track D: Retail Cashiers & Store Managers
- **Module 1**: POS Terminal Operation, Fast Scanning, and Barcode Lookup.
- **Module 2**: Multi-Payment Methods, Loyalty Programs, and Digital Signature Capture.
- **Module 3**: Cash Drawer Reconciliation and End-of-Day Shift Close.

---

## 2. Interactive Hands-on Labs & Validation
1. **Lab 1**: Create a new user, assign roles, and simulate a temporary JIT elevation request.
2. **Lab 2**: Execute a mock Disaster Recovery drill and verify the achieved RTO is strictly <= 4 hours.
3. **Lab 3**: Trigger a Point-in-Time recovery simulation to restore data to 10 minutes prior.
4. **Lab 4**: Create a sales order, checkout via POS terminal, capture digital signature, and inspect the resulting General Ledger journal entry.
