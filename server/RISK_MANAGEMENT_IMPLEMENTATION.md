# Risk Management Implementation

## Overview

This document describes the implementation of the Risk Management module for the Advanced Finance system. The module provides comprehensive financial risk assessment capabilities including currency risk, interest rate risk, and credit risk calculations, along with risk reporting and analytics.

**Task**: 32.4 - Implement risk management  
**Requirements**: 10.5 - WHEN risk exposure is assessed, THE System SHALL calculate currency, interest rate, and credit risk

## Features Implemented

### 1. Currency Risk Management

**Purpose**: Calculate and monitor foreign exchange risk exposure across all foreign currency positions.

**Capabilities**:
- Calculate total currency exposure by currency
- Separate asset and liability exposures
- Calculate net exposure (assets - liabilities)
- Support for currency hedges
- Value at Risk (VaR) calculation at 95% confidence level
- Potential loss estimation (10% adverse movement scenario)
- Exchange rate volatility analysis

**Data Sources**:
- Cash accounts in foreign currencies
- Investments in foreign currencies
- Loans (both receivable and payable) in foreign currencies
- Accounts Receivable invoices in foreign currencies
- Accounts Payable invoices in foreign currencies

**Risk Metrics**:
- **Total Exposure**: Sum of all assets and liabilities in a currency
- **Asset Exposure**: Total foreign currency assets
- **Liability Exposure**: Total foreign currency liabilities
- **Net Exposure**: Assets minus liabilities (positive = long position, negative = short position)
- **Value at Risk (VaR)**: Potential loss at 95% confidence level using historical volatility
- **Potential Loss**: Estimated loss under 10% adverse currency movement

### 2. Interest Rate Risk Management

**Purpose**: Assess exposure to interest rate changes across interest-bearing assets and liabilities.

**Capabilities**:
- Calculate total interest-bearing positions
- Compute weighted average interest rates
- Calculate duration gap (Macaulay duration)
- Basis Point Value (BPV) calculation
- Value at Risk for 100bp rate change
- Risk level assessment (low/medium/high)

**Data Sources**:
- Fixed-income investments (bonds, CDs, treasury bills)
- Loans receivable with interest
- Loans payable with interest

**Risk Metrics**:
- **Total Interest-Bearing Assets**: Sum of all interest-earning positions
- **Total Interest-Bearing Liabilities**: Sum of all interest-paying positions
- **Net Interest Position**: Assets minus liabilities
- **Average Asset Rate**: Weighted average interest rate on assets
- **Average Liability Rate**: Weighted average interest rate on liabilities
- **Duration Gap**: Difference between asset and liability durations
  - Positive gap: Assets mature after liabilities (rising rates = gain)
  - Negative gap: Assets mature before liabilities (rising rates = loss)
- **Basis Point Value (BPV)**: Change in portfolio value for 1bp rate change
- **Value at Risk**: Estimated loss for 100bp adverse rate movement
- **Risk Level**: 
  - Low: Duration gap < 1 year
  - Medium: Duration gap 1-3 years
  - High: Duration gap > 3 years

### 3. Credit Risk Management

**Purpose**: Evaluate credit exposure and potential losses from counterparty defaults.

**Capabilities**:
- Calculate total credit exposure
- Group exposure by counterparty
- Estimate probability of default (PD)
- Calculate expected credit loss (ECL)
- Calculate unexpected credit loss (Credit VaR)
- Concentration risk analysis
- Exposure by credit rating
- Risk level assessment

**Data Sources**:
- Accounts Receivable invoices (pending, partially paid, overdue)
- Loans receivable

**Risk Metrics**:
- **Total Credit Exposure**: Sum of all outstanding receivables and loans
- **Exposure by Counterparty**: Credit exposure grouped by customer/borrower
- **Probability of Default (PD)**: Estimated likelihood of counterparty default
  - Based on credit rating (if available)
  - Adjusted for overdue ratio
- **Expected Credit Loss (ECL)**: Exposure × PD × Loss Given Default (50%)
- **Unexpected Credit Loss**: Credit VaR at 95% confidence (ECL × 2.5)
- **Concentration Risk**: Percentage of exposure in top 10 counterparties
- **Risk Level**:
  - Low: ECL ratio < 2%
  - Medium: ECL ratio 2-5%
  - High: ECL ratio > 5%

### 4. Risk Reporting

**Purpose**: Generate comprehensive risk reports with actionable insights.

**Report Types**:
1. **Currency Risk Report**: Focus on foreign exchange exposures
2. **Interest Rate Risk Report**: Focus on interest rate sensitivity
3. **Credit Risk Report**: Focus on counterparty credit exposures
4. **Comprehensive Risk Report**: All risk types combined

**Report Contents**:
- Report metadata (ID, date, type, currency, generated by)
- Detailed risk metrics for each risk type
- Summary section with:
  - Total risk exposure (sum of all VaR metrics)
  - High-risk areas identified
  - Actionable recommendations

**Risk Dashboard**:
- Real-time risk metrics across all risk types
- Top 5 risks ranked by exposure
- Aggregate risk exposure
- Risk severity indicators

## Risk Calculation Methodologies

### Currency Risk - Value at Risk (VaR)

```
VaR = |Net Exposure| × Volatility × Z-score

Where:
- Net Exposure: Assets - Liabilities in foreign currency
- Volatility: Annualized standard deviation of exchange rate returns
- Z-score: 1.645 for 95% confidence level
```

### Interest Rate Risk - Duration Gap

```
Duration Gap = Asset Duration - Liability Duration

BPV = |Duration Gap| × Net Position × 0.0001
VaR = |Duration Gap| × Net Position × 0.01

Where:
- Duration: Macaulay duration (weighted average time to cash flows)
- Net Position: Interest-bearing assets - liabilities
- BPV: Basis Point Value (change for 1bp rate move)
- VaR: Value at Risk for 100bp rate change
```

### Credit Risk - Expected Credit Loss

```
ECL = Exposure × PD × LGD

Where:
- Exposure: Outstanding receivables + loans
- PD: Probability of Default (from rating or overdue ratio)
- LGD: Loss Given Default (assumed 50%)

Unexpected Loss (Credit VaR) = ECL × 2.5
```

## Best Practices

### 1. Regular Risk Assessment
- Run comprehensive risk reports monthly
- Monitor high-risk exposures daily
- Review risk dashboard before major decisions

### 2. Risk Mitigation Strategies
- **Currency Risk**: Use forward contracts, options, or natural hedges
- **Interest Rate Risk**: Match asset/liability durations, use swaps
- **Credit Risk**: Diversify customer base, implement credit limits, use insurance

### 3. Risk Limits
- Set maximum net currency exposure limits by currency
- Define acceptable duration gap ranges
- Establish concentration limits (e.g., max 10% exposure per counterparty)

### 4. Reporting Frequency
- **Daily**: Currency risk for major exposures
- **Weekly**: Credit risk for overdue accounts
- **Monthly**: Comprehensive risk report
- **Quarterly**: Board-level risk summary
