## Risk Calculation Methodologies

### Currency Risk - Value at Risk (VaR)

```
VaR = |Net Exposure| × Volatility × Z-score (1.645 for 95%)
```

- Uses historical exchange rate data to calculate volatility
- Annualized volatility based on 90-day rolling window
- 95% confidence level (Z-score = 1.645)

### Interest Rate Risk - Duration Gap

```
Duration Gap = Asset Duration - Liability Duration
BPV = |Duration Gap| × Net Position × 0.0001
VaR = |Duration Gap| × Net Position × 0.01
```

- Macaulay duration calculation for assets and liabilities
- Weighted by position size
- BPV shows sensitivity to 1 basis point change
- VaR shows impact of 100bp rate change

### Credit Risk - Expected Credit Loss

```
ECL = Exposure × PD × LGD
Unexpected Loss = ECL × 2.5
```

- PD (Probability of Default) from credit rating or overdue ratio
- LGD (Loss Given Default) assumed at 50%
- Unexpected loss at 95% confidence (2.5 × ECL)
